# Claims Requiring Owner Confirmation

The live homepage uses conservative, quotation-specific language until the points below are confirmed. Do not strengthen the copy or publish hard figures without evidence and an owner-approved operating statement.

## 1. Operating model and location

- Does Quantamorph manufacture directly, coordinate third-party manufacturing partners, or use a hybrid model?
- Which activities are performed by Quantamorph staff and which are performed by partners?
- In which countries can production take place?
- What wording may be used for the UK-facing commercial and technical relationship?
- May any office, partner facility or machine image be identified publicly?

## 2. Certified systems and standards

- ISO 9001 status: exact certified legal entity, scope, issuing body, certificate number and expiry.
- Any other QMS, welding, sector or product certifications.
- Whether partner certification may be referenced, and under which project conditions.

## 3. Machining and manufacturing capability

- CNC process list, machine types and axis counts.
- Turning, milling, conventional machining and reverse-engineering availability.
- Maximum work envelopes, part mass and fixturing limits.
- Tooling, press-tool, checking-fixture and production-fixture capability.
- Cutting, forming, fabrication, welding, assembly and automation capability.
- Prototype, low-volume and repeat-production quantity ranges.
- Capacity constraints and standard quotation/production lead-time ranges.

## 4. Materials and secondary processes

- Supported metals, polymers and material forms.
- Whether mill certificates or certificates of conformity can be supplied, and on what basis.
- Heat treatment, plating, coating, passivation, anodising and other finishes.
- Who controls and releases subcontracted special processes.

## 5. Tolerance and inspection

- Achievable tolerance capability by process, material, feature and geometry.
- Inspection equipment list, calibration controls and sampling policy.
- CMM availability and whether it is owned or partner-provided.
- Who identifies critical characteristics and who approves inspection plans.
- Whether “checked before release” accurately describes the standard operational workflow.

## 6. Documentation and traceability

- Standard inspection report and certificate package.
- FAI / first-article reporting availability and format.
- Material traceability, batch/lot identification and record-retention period.
- Drawing revision control, change control and release authority.
- Delivery, conformity and packing documentation supplied by default versus by agreement.

## 7. Confidentiality and drawing control

- NDA availability and authorised signatory.
- Secure file-transfer method and accepted file formats/sizes.
- Drawing/CAD access, storage location, encryption, retention, reuse and deletion controls.
- Whether confidential drawings may be submitted to `sales@quantamorph.co.uk` without prior agreement.

## 8. Delivery and commercial coverage

- Countries routinely served.
- Incoterms, customs responsibilities and import/export handling.
- Packaging standards and any component-preservation requirements.
- Whether a named point of contact and project-status communication are standard commitments.

## 9. Sectors and proof

- For Automotive, Electrical, Hydraulics, Construction, Agriculture, Industrial Equipment and Special-purpose Machinery, identify whether each is:
  - completed project experience;
  - an active, qualified capability; or
  - a target sector only.
- Permission to publish case studies, component photographs, testimonials or customer marks.
- Rights and provenance for every supplied image, including visible third-party trademarks.

## 10. Administrative details

- Confirm the sales email as the intended public RFQ route.
- Approve legal, privacy, cookie and website terms before public launch.
- The current [Companies House record](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/17389114) confirms the legal name, company number and registered office; the owner should still approve the public footer presentation.

## 11. Fort Automation profile integration

- Confirm the commercial relationship between Quantamorph Limited and Fort Automation, including whether Fort may be named publicly as a partner.
- Confirm permission to reproduce the special-purpose-machine, checking-fixture, application-fixture and reverse-engineering images from the supplied profile.
- Confirm which Fort capabilities may be offered through Quantamorph: CAD development, legacy 2D conversion, GD&T drafting, progressive-tool design, SPM design/build, checking fixtures, gauges, CMM part-holding fixtures, welding fixtures, reverse engineering, press tools/dies and metrology workstations.
- Confirm who owns design authority, CE/UKCA and machinery-safety responsibilities for special-purpose-machine projects.
- Confirm how validation, run-off, acceptance testing, manuals, risk assessment and after-sales support are scoped for automation projects.
- Confirm the intellectual-property and authorisation checks required before accepting reverse-engineering work.
- Do not present the customer list in the Fort profile as Quantamorph customers. Obtain permission and clarify the relationship before publishing any names or logos.
